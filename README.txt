CONNECT V825 — CROWN & MAX

1. Extract this entire ZIP.
2. Open Connect_V825_CROWN_MAX.html in your browser.
3. Open the small bot-controls button at the bottom right.
4. Under Your companion, choose CROWN or MAX.
5. Drag the small arrow handle at the bot's upper-left to change his size.
   Drag the character itself to move him. Sizes range from 128 to 360 pixels.
6. Drag the corner handle on the text box to resize it. Use the Left/Right
   button in its header to swap sides. Settings also offer Auto placement.

Size and placement are remembered in this browser. Keyboard users can focus
either resize handle and use arrow keys (Shift makes larger adjustments).
Escape cancels an active resize. Reset size and placement restores defaults
without changing your character or conversation. Smaller screens temporarily
fit the bot and text box to the available space.

The selected character is remembered in this browser. Switching characters
keeps the same conversation, tasks, rep, and trigger assignments. Both characters
have 36 supplied animations and PNG posters. Pause and reduced-motion settings
use the posters. Bubble sound remains optional and starts off; switching
characters turns it off again.

Keep the assets folder beside the HTML file. Both original characters' supplied
WebP animations, PNG posters, and optional bubble sound are included unchanged.
Uploaded custom animations remain shared and play the artwork you uploaded.
This is the existing local demonstration, with simulated connections.
